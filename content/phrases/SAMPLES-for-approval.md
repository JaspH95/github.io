# Phrase pack samples (for Jasper's approval)

These are 20 sample phrases per language, in the order the pack will teach them: easiest and most useful first. Once you approve a language, I'll write its full pack (about 120 phrases across the 11 themes in `CLAUDE.md`) into `content/phrases/<language>.json`. Every phrase will start with `checked: false` until a native speaker confirms it.

"Say it" is an approximate English-sounding guide. Capitals mark the stressed syllable.

## Spanish (Spain)

| # | Phrase | Say it | Meaning | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hola | OH-lah | Hello | Any time, anyone |
| 2 | Buenos días | BWEH-nos DEE-as | Good morning | Until about 2pm in Spain |
| 3 | ¿Qué tal? | keh TAL | How's it going? | Casual hello |
| 4 | Me llamo… | meh YAH-moh | My name is… | Introducing yourself |
| 5 | Encantado / Encantada | en-kan-TAH-doh / -dah | Nice to meet you | Men say -o, women say -a |
| 6 | Por favor | por fah-VOR | Please | Everywhere |
| 7 | Gracias | GRAH-thyas | Thank you | In Spain the c sounds like "th" |
| 8 | De nada | deh NAH-dah | You're welcome | Reply to thanks |
| 9 | Perdón | per-DON | Sorry, excuse me | Bumping into someone, getting past |
| 10 | No pasa nada | noh PAH-sah NAH-dah | No worries | When someone apologises for something small |
| 11 | ¿Me pones un café, por favor? | meh POH-nes oon kah-FEH | Can I get a coffee, please? | Ordering at a bar in Spain (normal, not rude) |
| 12 | La cuenta, por favor | lah KWEN-tah | The bill, please | End of a meal |
| 13 | ¿Cuánto cuesta? | KWAN-toh KWES-tah | How much is it? | Shopping |
| 14 | ¿Dónde está el baño? | DON-deh es-TAH el BAH-nyoh | Where's the toilet? | Anywhere |
| 15 | No entiendo | noh en-TYEN-doh | I don't understand | When you're lost in the conversation |
| 16 | ¿Hablas inglés? | AH-blas een-GLES | Do you speak English? | Informal; "¿Habla inglés?" is formal |
| 17 | Más despacio, por favor | mas des-PAH-thyoh | More slowly, please | When someone talks fast |
| 18 | ¡Qué rico! | keh REE-koh | Delicious! | About food you're enjoying |
| 19 | Hasta luego | AS-tah LWEH-goh | See you later | Common goodbye, even to strangers |
| 20 | ¿Qué hora es? | keh OH-rah es | What time is it? | Asking the time |
