About Knowfeed

Last updated 5 October 2026

Knowfeed is a personal newspaper and learning app that ends. News arrives in Morning, Midday and Evening editions that finish, and when you're up to date, learning takes over: skills for your work, your interests, and a language.

Who makes it

Knowfeed is made and run by Jasper Hayward, an individual based in the United Kingdom. It is not a registered company. Knowfeed is currently a free, non-commercial beta for a small group of testers: there are no adverts, no payments and no in-app purchases.

Contact

For questions, feedback, privacy requests or anything else, email jasperhayward@me.com. You can also send feedback from inside the app: Profile, then More settings, then Feedback, or "Something wrong?" on any card.

Where the content comes from

  • News comes from news outlets' own public feeds and the Guardian's Open Platform. Every story links to the original, and summaries are written only from the outlets' own text. Headlines and summaries belong to their publishers.
  • Learning comes from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA), NASA, Wikidata, the EU's ESCO skills classification, Open Library and Project Gutenberg, always with a link to the source.
  • Story summaries are drafted by AI (Google's Gemini) from the articles' text, checked automatically for names and numbers that don't appear in the source, and clearly labelled. They can still contain mistakes, so check the original for anything important.
  • Images come from the articles themselves, Wikimedia Commons, Unsplash or Pexels, and are credited on each card.

Accessibility

Knowfeed aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA: screen reader labels, keyboard access, text that follows your device's text size, and colour contrast that meets AA. If something doesn't work for you, please email jasperhayward@me.com and it will be fixed.
