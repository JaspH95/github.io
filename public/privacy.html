Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

The short version

  • Most of what Knowfeed knows about you stays on your phone.
  • If you create an account, a copy is kept in Knowfeed's database so it survives a new phone.
  • No adverts, no selling or sharing your data, no third-party trackers and no cookies.
  • Usage stats are only collected if you say yes, and you can turn them off any time.
  • You can see, export or delete everything, from inside the app.

Who is responsible for your data

Knowfeed is run by Jasper Hayward, an individual in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: jasperhayward@me.com.

What Knowfeed keeps, and why

1. On your phone

To build your editions, Knowfeed stores your answers on your device: your first name, your town or city, your work and the skills you want to build, your interests, languages, sports and teams, the outlets you follow, your edition times and quiet hours, and any reading goal or limit. It also stores what you like, save and follow, what you've already read (so you don't see it again), your learning progress, and feedback notes. This stays on your phone and isn't sent to us unless you create an account. Lawful basis: providing the service you asked for (contract).

2. If you create an account

Your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our database provider, Supabase), plus a copy of the information above so it can be restored on another phone. A few of your answers (first name, city, time zone, job, edition times, quiet hours and any reading goal or limit) are also kept as plain fields so the beta can be run and improved. Your email is only used to sign you in. We never send marketing emails. Lawful basis: contract.

3. Usage stats, only if you allow them

If you choose "Allow usage stats", Knowfeed counts things like editions opened and finished, stories opened, likes, saves and lessons finished, with the time. For signed-in testers these are sent to Knowfeed's own database, to see which features help people and whether testers come back. If you choose "No thanks", or later turn stats off in Profile, then Your data and privacy, nothing is counted, and anything already counted is deleted from your phone and your account. Lawful basis: consent.

4. Feedback you send

If you're signed in and send feedback, we keep what you wrote and which card it was about, to fix problems. Lawful basis: legitimate interests (improving the app).

5. Searches and lookups

When you search, type in a city, team or interest, or open live scores, the words you type are sent to Knowfeed's server functions to fetch results from news and data services. They aren't stored or linked to you. Lawful basis: contract.

6. Technical information

Like any website, our host (Vercel) receives your IP address and basic request details when the app loads or calls our server functions, and keeps them briefly in logs for security and fixing faults. Lawful basis: legitimate interests (keeping the service secure and working).

Knowfeed does not knowingly collect any special category data (such as health, religion or politics). Your interests are only used to choose what goes in your editions, never to profile you for advertising.

International transfers

Vercel and Supabase are US companies and may process data outside the UK. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses in their data processing terms.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete it (Profile, then Your data and privacy, then Delete everything) or remove the app. Stories you've seen are remembered for 7 days, and those you've read or hidden for 21 days.
  • Your account and everything linked to it: until you delete your account, which removes it straight away.
  • Usage stats: until you turn them off or delete your account.
  • Server logs: kept by Vercel for a short time (days, not months).

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy (data portability), object to or restrict how it's used, and withdraw consent at any time. Much of this is built into the app:

  • See and change your answers: Profile, or Talk to Knowfeed.
  • Export a copy: Profile, then Your data and privacy, then Back up to a file.
  • Delete everything: Profile, then Your data and privacy, then Delete everything. If you're signed in, this deletes your account and all data linked to it.
  • Turn usage stats off: Profile, then Your data and privacy.

For anything else, or if you can't use the app, email jasperhayward@me.com. We'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

Children

Knowfeed is for people aged 13 and over, and asks your age before you start. If you're under 13, Knowfeed won't set itself up and nothing is saved. If we learn that we hold data about a child under 13, we'll delete it.

Security

Everything is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). In the database, each person can only read and change their own data (row-level security), and passwords are never stored in readable form.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. If it's a significant change, such as collecting something new, we'll tell you in the app first.
