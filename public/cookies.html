Cookie Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026

The short version

  • Knowfeed doesn't use cookies.
  • It saves your settings and progress in your phone's local storage, because the app can't work without it.
  • Usage stats are the only optional part, and they're off unless you allow them.

Cookies

Knowfeed does not set any cookies, and doesn't load any advertising, analytics or social media tools that would. Fonts are built into the app, so nothing is requested from Google Fonts.

What is stored on your device

Instead of cookies, Knowfeed uses your browser's local storage and offline cache. UK law (PECR) allows storage that's strictly necessary to provide a service you've asked for. Everything below is strictly necessary, except usage stats, which need your consent.

Changing your choice

When you first open Knowfeed, it asks whether to allow usage stats. Both answers are equally easy. You can change your mind any time in Profile, then Your data and privacy. Turning stats off also deletes anything already counted.

You can remove everything Knowfeed stores with Profile, then Your data and privacy, then Delete everything, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
